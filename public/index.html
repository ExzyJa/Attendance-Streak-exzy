01 / The rhythm

One reaction.
A visible promise.

01

Check in daily

At your chosen time, the bot posts a fresh attendance message. React with ✅ to keep your place.

02

Build your run

Every check-in extends a personal streak. Shields can cover a single missed day without breaking the momentum.

03

Let the roles speak

When someone misses too long, the bot moves them to inactive and can announce the change in a channel you choose.

03 / Ready when you are

Midnight in Manila.
Momentum everywhere.

Use the values below to start a daily attendance ritual for your server.

SETUP / ASIA-MANILA
channel: #attendance
announcement-channel: #attendance-alerts
time: 00:00
timezone: Asia/Manila
enable-role-automation: true
active-role: @Active
inactive-role: @Inactive
exemption-roles: @Staff, @Admin

Need a different reset? Keep the timezone and change time to any 24-hour value.
